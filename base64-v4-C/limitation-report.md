The helper-only maximum is 13,835,058,055,282,163,709. D(m) fits usize for usize m, but not every such capacity is a legal single slice. This is a symbolic representation argument, not an allocation promise or verification of actual helper implementations. Existing adequate buffers are premises; practical RAM limits do not narrow the promised byte domain.

| Exact prefixes/counts and unchanged inputs | Encoder writes exactly encoding(B) and reports E(n); decoder consumes exactly that reported prefix, reports n and recovers bytes in order without loss/duplication; preservation of both borrowed inputs is unproved. |
| Both complete destination frames | Encode suffix and decode suffix preservation, independence from initial destination bytes and retention of encoded storage through decoding remain unproved. |
| Helpers and representation | Concrete STANDARD/table/config witness, padding, encode/decode internals and every material helper’s sufficiency/contract implementation remain unproved. |
| Successful public composition | The source-grounded caller is not verified: capacities, returned-prefix slicing, byte equality, lifecycle frames and absence of unjustified errors, overflow/index/debug-assertion or other codec panics remain open. |

B denotes the bytes returned by the wrapper’s single actual AsRef invocation, not a second conversion or a pre-state pure view. Sizing from an earlier conversion needs stability or a justified bound on the future observation. Arbitrary user conversion or Drop can mutate unrelated state, panic or diverge. Conditional codec reasoning after a valid conversion and total public-call guarantees therefore require distinct, justified user-code/environment interfaces. This observation is not permission to narrow arbitrary byte inputs or assume generic purity. The byte-slice representative [caller](/fastdata/v-boxiuli/noah/exp/tsC/verification/.system-proof/t1/caller.rs) does not replace the generic public obligation.

The bounded imported checked_add contribution and D1 scope observations do not supply that capability. The Manager stopped compiler expansion beyond the authorized scope; this is the stopping reason, not a proof that every alternative route is impossible.
